# Adonai

*Working title.* A creative, faithful dramatization of the Bible that expresses your understanding of Christianity. It gets written first as story and screenplay, then produced as a fully rendered 3D animated series, which is the placeholder for an eventual live-action version. It uses modern dialogue so that ancient people feel as present to us as they did to the people who first heard their stories.

This directory holds the planning documents, the Opening's research and treatment, the Ruth pilot's research, treatment and beat sheet, and the first-draft Ruth script.

## The short version

**Modern words, ancient minds.** Characters speak contemporary English, so the audience hears them as people rather than statues. They think, want and know only what people of their own time could have.

**Everything has a provenance.** Every element of a scene is labeled by where it came from: Scripture, history, tradition, scholarly inference, your own conviction, or invention. Every line of dialogue is labeled by how much freedom was taken with it. The words the text records, above all from God and from Jesus, are the default: changing their substance takes a discussion first.

**Make it land the way it landed.** For every scene we ask what the first audience knew and felt that a modern audience doesn't. Then we get that across through staging and story, not through lectures.

**Pilot first.** The proposed pilot is Ruth: small, full of dialogue, rich in cultural detail and low in theological risk. It gets written all the way through before we lay out the full series.

## What I need from you

### Now: the Opening (Genesis 1:1–2:7)

Your answers to the earlier eight are recorded in the [dossier §7](./story/arcs/01-beginnings/opening/DOSSIER.md#7-your-choices-decided).

1. **Your notes on [treatment v2](./story/arcs/01-beginnings/opening/TREATMENT.md).** Eleven things in it are my suggestions and not yours. They're listed in [§5](./story/arcs/01-beginnings/opening/TREATMENT.md#5-whats-mine-and-three-problems) so you can strike any of them.
2. **Genesis 1:11–13.** You offered two readings. I've taken the second, the matter itself growing into many forms, and shown it as the gas pulling into branching threads with buds at the crossings, which then light as the stars. Yes or no?
3. **The rest of your reading: 1:20–2:3.** You stopped at the stars. My defaults: life in the sea, then the land, then people, then the cliff as the seventh day. One gap: the flying things of 1:20 come before the land animals, and nothing flies in the sequence. Add wings over the shore, or leave it?
4. **The breath.** You said the person at the cliff is Adam's ancestor and that the face takes one breath. But 2:7 is God breathing into Adam. I've had the ancestor draw the breath in and Adam let it out in the Garden's first shot. Or should the whole breath stay at the cliff, or the Opening end at 2:6?
5. **The flicker.** A full frame flashing black and white faster than three times a second can trigger seizures. I've had the alternation break into smaller and smaller patches as it speeds up, which is also how it becomes the sea of particles. Is that all right?
6. **Is the malice really in God?** You said all four faces are God. Malice as part of God contradicts 1 John 1:5 and James 1:13. It fits if the dark face is how the whole looks to a part that's been cut off, which is what you said about separation: "his own illusion. His own perception." Is that what you mean? ([Discussion 007](./discussions/007-the-faces-of-god.md), question 1.)

### Discussion 007: the faces of God

7. **Who are the large face and the small one?** The whole and a part? God and what he's made? Two persons of the Trinity? If the dark half is the Father and the light half the Son, the picture says something the texts rule out.
8. **"Fundamental duality creates God."** Does God need the duality in order to be? Or is there a two-in-one inside God from eternity, with the created duality as its image?
9. **Does the first face recur?** Is it Adam's face, and in the end Jesus's? And is it a man's face, a woman's, or one that could be either?

### Discussion 001: how God speaks, round 2

Round 1 is [recorded](./discussions/001-how-god-speaks.md#round-1--2026-10-03), and four points are settled.

10. **God speaking to someone alone.** My proposal from your answers: the world goes quiet, the Opening's held note returns, and the words come in the hearer's own voice, steadier than their own, and able to say what they don't want to hear. Does that fit?
11. **"Prophets are God a lot of the time."** The prophets and apostles refuse that sentence every time it's put to them (Acts 14:15; Rev 22:9). Is "the prophet takes part in God, and God is the one speaking" what you mean?
12. **"Illusion."** Is "the separation is real as experience and never real as fact" a fair statement of it?
13. **The visions** of Isaiah, Ezekiel and Daniel: are they where the Opening's world comes back at full strength?
