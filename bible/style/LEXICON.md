# Lexicon

> Term-by-term rules: which words we use, which we avoid, and why. Started with Ruth; it grows with every arc and feeds the script checker later (ROADMAP §13.5).

**Status:** v1.1, 2026-10-10 (adds "kind," not "species"). v1, 2026-10-02: built from the Ruth script. The general rules are in ROADMAP §5; this file is the working list.

---

## God

| Say | Don't say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "God," "the Lord" | — | The default (ROADMAP §5.5) |
| "Yahweh" | anywhere but an oath, or where the Name itself is the point | Kept rare and weighty. In Ruth: 1:17 and 3:13 only. |
| "the Almighty" | "Shaddai," "El Shaddai" | Naomi's old name for God at the well (1:20–21) |
| "Chemosh" | "Kemosh," "the Moabite god" | Moab's god, by name (Num 21:29) |
| "the goddess," "her" | a name for the goddess | We don't know which goddess a Moabite household figure was. |

**Swearing:** no sacred names as curses (ROADMAP §5.4, category 1). Oaths are the opposite of swearing: "May Yahweh do that to me, and worse," "as Yahweh lives."

## Adam and Eve

| Say | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "kind," "of one kind" | "species" | The change in them isn't bodily (the author, 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08; CLAUDE.md) |

## Family and law

| Say | Avoid | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| "family" | "kin," "kinsman" in dialogue | "That man is family." "Kinsman" is fine in the old elder's formula, which is meant to sound legal. |
| "redeemer" | "kinsman-redeemer" in dialogue | The plot word. It gets one clear beat when it first appears (2:20). |
| "buy it back," "redeem it" | "exercise the right of redemption" | |
| "my daughter," "my daughters" | overusing them | Keep one where it carries weight (STORYTELLING). |
| "mother-in-law" | — | Boaz's word (2:11). Ruth calls her nothing, or "you." |
| "the Moabite" | "the foreigner" as a label | It's what the town calls her, and the label drops away after 4:10, as in the text. |
| "Mr. So-and-so" | any name for him | Credits only. On screen his name is drowned out (4:1). |
| "a husband," "rest" | "security," "a household" | 1:9 and 3:1 |
| "takes care of him," "carries him" | "nurse" | *Omenet* (4:16) means a guardian, not a wet nurse. |

## Harvest and land

| Say | Avoid | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| "glean," "gleaners" | "pick up leftovers" | The legal right is the point (Lev 19:9–10). |
| "sheaves," "stalks," "reapers," "threshing floor," "winnow" | — | Concrete farm words are fine and timeless. |
| "a measure," "six measures" | "pounds," "kilos," "bushels" in dialogue | Units are for the action lines: "about an ephah, around thirty pounds." |
| "silver," "shekels' weight of silver" | "coins," "money" | There are no coins until centuries later; silver is weighed (ROADMAP §5.4, category 4). "Pay" is fine: "what somebody will pay." |
| "the use of the field" | "lease," "rent" | |
| "the harvest" | "summer job," "season" in a modern sense | |

## Time

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "tonight," "at dawn," "before light," "midnight" | "hours," "minutes," "o'clock" |
| "the barley harvest," "the wheat harvest," "the plowing" | month names |

## Words that sound right but aren't

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "Jew," "Jewish" | Centuries too early. Say "Israelite" or "from Judah." |
| "Bible," "Scripture," "religion" | Nobody in Ruth has a Bible, and "religion" is a modern category. "Their god," "how they pray" instead. |
| "Palestine," "the Holy Land" | Anachronistic. "Judah," "Israel," "the land." |
| "Goodbye" as a word on its own | Fine in principle, but the characters' own blessings are better: "God be with you." |
| "Look," "Listen," "So," "Well" as openers | Written-sounding (STORYTELLING) |
| "OK," "deal," "fine" as agreement | Too modern in this mouth. "Yes." "Good." Or a nod. |
| Therapy words: "process," "closure," "trauma," "boundaries" | ROADMAP §5.4, category 5 |

## Animals and things

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Roosters, chickens | Rare in the region this early (fixed in the sample scene: a dog barks instead) |
| Glass windows, candles, chairs at a table | Iron Age houses: small openings, oil lamps, mats and low stools |
| Coins | See above |
