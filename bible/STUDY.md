## Start here

My picks if you watch only four, in this order:

1. **The Tree of Life**, the creation sequence: the closest thing on film to the Opening's first eleven beats.
2. **Apocalypto**, the chase: the run you're laying out now.
3. **Arcane**, season 1: the series' base look, or its fallback (README item 15).
4. **The Chosen**, season 1: biblical people in modern speech, the series' register, with its risks.

## The Opening: from the deep to the land (beats 1–11)

- **The Tree of Life** (2011) · film · Terrence Malick · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tree_of_Life_(film))
  A family in 1950s Texas, set against the making of the universe. *Study:* the creation sequence early in the film, made by Douglas Trumbull with dyes, milk and paint in tanks shot at up to 1,000 frames a second. It's the benchmark for cosmic images that never look computer-made.
- **Voyage of Time** (2016) · documentary · Terrence Malick · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyage_of_Time)
  The same team's follow-up: the universe from its beginning to early life. *Study:* the closest film to beats 6–11. There are two cuts, a short IMAX one narrated by Brad Pitt and the feature-length *Life's Journey* narrated by Cate Blanchett.
- **Emergence** (2016–19) · audiovisual film · Max Cooper · [maxcooper.net](https://maxcooper.net/emergence)
  A wordless 90-minute film that runs from physics through the origins of life to people. *Study:* music and picture built as one thing, the Opening's rule for sound.
- **Fractal Time** (2019) and **Recombination** (2023) · fulldome shorts · Julius Horsthuis · [Fractal Time](https://www.idfa.nl/en/film/873bf445-df4a-42e2-8eb9-73e7efe380c6/fractal-time), [Recombination](https://www.nordische-filmtage.de/en/festival/movie/view/2175)
  Fractal landscapes that grow and fold without end. *Study:* the vault (beat 5) and the fractal feel you liked in the middle beats.

## The Opening: the people and the run (beat 13)

The scene notes for these are from my memory of the films; check each scene before leaning on a detail.

- **Apocalypto** (2006) · film · Mel Gibson · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apocalypto)
  A Maya man runs for his life through the jungle from the men who captured him. *Study:* the chase in the second half: the camera at shin height in the undergrowth, the hunted and the hunters cut together so you always know how close they are, and a jaguar in it too.
- **Prey** (2022) · film · Dan Trachtenberg · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prey_(2022_film))
  A young Comanche woman hunts, and is hunted, on the plains in 1719. *Study:* a pursuer gaining in wide shots, with the camera at her level in forest and long grass.
- **The Revenant** (2015) · film · Alejandro G. Iñárritu, shot by Emmanuel Lubezki · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Revenant_(2015_film))
  A frontiersman left for dead in the wilderness of the 1820s. *Study:* long moving takes in natural light, and the opening attack in the forest, where the camera roves among people close enough to touch.
- **The Last of the Mohicans** (1992) · film · Michael Mann · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_the_Mohicans_(1992_film))
  The French and Indian War on the colonial frontier. *Study:* the runs through the forest, with long lenses from fixed places panning as runners flick between the trunks: our planted cameras.
- **Predator** (1987) · film · John McTiernan · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predator_(film))
  Soldiers in a jungle hunted by something they can't see. *Study:* runners glimpsed through foreground foliage, with a long lens flattening the jungle.
- **The Lost World: Jurassic Park** (1997) · film · Steven Spielberg · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_World:_Jurassic_Park)
  The sequel's expedition to a second island. *Study:* the long-grass scene, seen from above as lines in the grass close in on the runners: how fast a pursuer is, without showing it.
- **Raiders of the Lost Ark** (1981) · film · Steven Spielberg · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raiders_of_the_Lost_Ark)
  The first Indiana Jones film. *Study:* the opening run from the Hovitos: a runner coming at a wide lens with the pursuers spilling out of the trees behind.
- **Planet of the Apes** (1968) · film · Franklin J. Schaffner · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_of_the_Apes_(1968_film))
  Astronauts land on a planet ruled by apes. *Study:* the hunt in the cornfield, a whole group scattering through tall growth with nobody in formation.
- **Quest for Fire** (1981) · film · Jean-Jacques Annaud · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quest_for_Fire_(film))
  Early humans set out to find fire, told without any language we can understand (the sounds by Anthony Burgess, the body language by Desmond Morris). *Study:* people who shout and call without words, as the band does.
- **Persistence Hunting** · documentary clip · BBC, *The Life of Mammals*, with David Attenborough · [CyberTracker](https://cybertracker.org/video-persistence-hunting/)
  A San tracker runs a kudu down in the Kalahari heat. *Study:* the gait, breath and heat of a long run through open country: the grassland stretch.

## The series' look

- **Arcane** (2021–24) · animated series · Fortiche, for Riot Games · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcane_(TV_series))
  Two sisters on opposite sides of a city split between rich and poor. *Study:* 3D underneath with a painted surface, real space and light. It's the look [Discussion 006](./discussions/006-2d-or-3d.md) chose for the series' base style; whether it stays the base or becomes the fallback to realism is open ([README](./README.md#what-i-need-from-you), item 15).
- **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** (2018) · animated film · Bob Persichetti, Peter Ramsey, Rodney Rothman · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Into_the_Spider-Verse)
  A Brooklyn teenager becomes Spider-Man and meets others from other worlds. *Study:* the register for visions and the spiritual (Discussion 006): vibrant, graphic, free to break the frame.

## The Bible on screen

How others have told these stories. What each gets right and wrong for us is in [RULES §2.3](./canon/RULES.md#23-precedents).

- **The Chosen** (2019– ) · series · Dallas Jenkins · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chosen_(TV_series))
  The life of Jesus through the people who met him. *Study:* modern conversational dialogue making biblical people vivid, and how much invented backstory that takes.
- **The Prince of Egypt** (1998) · animated film · DreamWorks · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prince_of_Egypt)
  The Exodus, from Moses' birth to Sinai. *Study:* animation carrying real weight and spectacle; its honest opening note on the liberties it takes; and its central invention, Moses and Rameses raised as brothers, as the kind of change to weigh carefully.
- **The Passion of the Christ** (2004) · film · Mel Gibson · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Passion_of_the_Christ)
  The last twelve hours of Jesus' life, spoken in Aramaic, Hebrew and Latin. *Study:* the historical texture the languages give; and, as cautions, its prolonged violence and its scenes drawn from later devotional visions.
- **The Ten Commandments** (1956) · film · Cecil B. DeMille · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ten_Commandments_(1956_film))
  The life of Moses as a Hollywood epic. *Study:* scale and awe; and the stiff, archaic dialogue that turns people into a museum piece ([RULES §2.4](./canon/RULES.md#24-three-ways-to-fail)).
- **The King of Kings** (2025) · animated film · Seong-ho Jang · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King_of_Kings_(2025_film))
  Charles Dickens tells his young son the life of Jesus. *Study:* a recent animated Gospel, and its frame story ([RULES §10.2](./canon/RULES.md#102-how-to-structure-it)).

## How the list is kept

- **Sections are by what a title is for,** not by medium, and each entry says its medium. New sections open as titles need them (books, music, games, places to visit).
- **Each entry:** the title and year, the medium, the maker, one link (Wikipedia where there's a page, otherwise the maker's or publisher's), a line on what it is without spoilers, and what to study it for.
- **One entry per title.** If a title serves two purposes it sits in the first and the line says the second.
- **The descriptions are mine,** in a sentence or two, never copied from a review or a blurb.
