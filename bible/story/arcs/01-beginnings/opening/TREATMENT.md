# The Opening — Treatment

> Genesis 1:1–2:7. One unbroken sequence, 4 minutes 35 seconds, no words.

**Status:** v1, 2026-10-03. It follows your *Scene Descriptions* draft beat for beat. It's written on my defaults for the eight choices in the [dossier](./DOSSIER.md#7-choices-for-you), so anything you answer differently changes it. Changes from your draft are listed in §3.

**Pace.** It starts slow and strange, and speeds up at every beat from 5 to 10: each one covers more time in less screen. The two places it stops are the leaf (two seconds) and the cliff (twenty-five). There are no cuts to black inside it. Each beat hands over to the next through something we're already looking at: the particle, the photon, the cell, the creature, the carcass, the leaf.

**One thing is followed all the way through.** A face becomes a particle, the particle becomes a proton, the proton gives off a photon, the photon feeds a cell, the cell's line becomes an animal, the animal passes a body, the body becomes a tree, the tree drops a leaf, people run over the leaf, and one of them has the face.

## 2. The beats

### 1. The deep (0:00–0:20)

Black and white static fills the frame. The sound is a hiss, but not an electrical one: it's closer to wind over open water. [[P1: 1:2, the Spirit, *ruach*, wind or breath, hovering over the face of the waters.]]

The static begins to flow. It moves across the frame like a current, with eddies in it. Where it flows, something stands out of it: the imprint of a human face, expressionless, eyes closed. Nothing draws it. The static inside it simply moves differently from the static around it, and if the current stopped the face would vanish. [[P1: 1:2, "the face of the deep." P5: your image.]]

### 2. Darkness (0:20–0:40)

Something pushes the static outward. It pours away from the face on every side and wraps around behind it, and where it leaves, there's nothing: flat black. The face is left alone, a face of static in the dark. The hiss thins to almost nothing. [[P1: 1:2, "darkness was over the face of the deep."]]

The static in the face slows and whitens until the face is pure white. As it does, the eyes open, and the mouth, a little.

The darkness around it isn't empty any more. It gathers into a second face, far larger, made of black on black and seen only by its edges, and what it shows is malice. The white face sees it. Fear, then terror. [[P5: your framing, FR-01. Tension: see dossier §4.2. The malice appears only after the face is cut off.]]

### 3. The separation (0:40–0:50)

The dark face comes for the white one. Both expressions reach their height.

At the instant it would land, the image mirrors. No impact. Where there was one white face held in darkness, there is now also a dark face held in white, and the line between the two halves is a single curve. The first sound with any force in it: one low tone. [[P1: 1:4, "God separated the light from the darkness"; John 1:5, "the darkness has not overcome it."]]

### 4. Evening and morning (0:50–1:10)

The view pulls back. The two halves, light and dark, sit together as one round figure turning slowly in a sea of static. [[Choice 3: the shape is evoked, not drawn.]]

Four faces now. In the dark half, the malicious face around the terrified one. In the light half, a contented face around a discontented one.

As the figure turns, two things happen in it, over and over. In the dark half, the large face hurts the small one. In the light half, the large face heals the small one. Separation, connection. Each time the figure comes round, they happen again. [[P5: your framing. P1: 1:5, "there was evening and there was morning."]]

The turning speeds up. The faces smear. Dark and light blur into one even grey, and the figure is a single round particle. The view keeps pulling back: every grain of the static is one of these.

### 5. Light (1:10–1:40)

The particle is restless. It darts, doubles back, never still. It meets two others, and the three lock into a tight knot, circling one another so fast they read as one body: a proton. [[P2: three quarks. P5: your "trinity."]]

A smaller, quicker thing, an electron, falls in around it. All through the field the same pairing happens at once, and the haze that filled the frame clears. Light runs free through it for the first time. [[P2: recombination. P1: 1:3.]]

The gas isn't even. It drifts together, thickens, falls inward and ignites. We're inside a star, and it's violent: the electron is torn away, and bare protons slam past each other in the glare. Ours hits another and holds. It hits a third. From that last collision a photon comes off. [[P2: proton–proton fusion to deuterium, then helium-3 and a gamma ray. P1: 1:14–18.]]

### 6. The crossing (1:40–1:55)

We go with the photon. It fights its way out through the star, breaks the surface, and then there's nothing in its way at all. Silence, speed, black.

Ahead, a point of dull red: a world still molten. As we close on it, it cools. Crust, cloud, then ocean, blue-grey from edge to edge, with bare dark land standing out of it. [[P1: 1:9–10. P2 with license: one photon, and the Earth's first billion years seen in its approach.]]

Down through cloud, through the surface of the sea, into green-lit water, and into a single cell. It takes the light in.

### 7. The waters swarm (1:55–2:35)

The cell divides. Again. Again. Generations go by in seconds, and we stay with one line of descent each time.

Something much larger closes around our cell and swallows it whole. It isn't digested. It settles inside and goes on dividing in step with its host, and the two are one creature. [[P2: endosymbiosis.]]

These divide and hold together: clumps, sheets, bodies. A body with a front and a back. A small, soft, swimming thing with a rod down its spine, in a sea now crowded with life. [[P1: 1:20–21. P2: *Haikouichthys*, then *Pikaia*.]]

Fish. Bigger fish behind it. It gets away.

### 8. The land (2:35–3:05)

Shallows, mud, air. Fins that push like legs. The creature hauls out onto a shore where nothing has walked before.

It's lizard-shaped now, low and quick, among ferns. Then small, furred and sharp-nosed, living at the feet of enormous animals, at night. [[P2: early amniotes; shrew-like early mammals. P1: 1:24–25.]]

A light in the sky that shouldn't be there. Then the impact, far away: a flash below the horizon, a wall of wind, fire falling. The small creature runs for a hole in the ground. On its way it passes a great animal lying dead, and it doesn't stop. We do.

### 9. Dust (3:05–3:25)

We stay on the body, and time runs. Ash falls on it. The flesh goes, then the bones settle into the ground. The sky clears. Around it the land greens, changes, greens differently. Animals pass as blurs.

Beside where the body lay, a shoot. It's a sapling, then a tree, in a few seconds. [[P1: 1:11–12, and 2:7, "dust from the ground."]]

One leaf comes loose and falls into frame. It settles on the earth.

Everything is still. Two seconds.

### 10. Fill the earth (3:25–4:10)

Feet. A crowd of them, running, straight over the leaf. People, screaming, wild. We go with them.

A band of early humans, men and women, with spears and tools, in forest. Something is chasing them, or they're chasing something. [[P1: 1:27, "male and female he created them."]]

From here the people keep changing while the movement never stops: the band is always heading the same way, north, and each time the view passes behind a tree or a rise, it's their children, then theirs. The forest thins to open woodland and then to grassland. They fight a big cat. They fight another band, and it's ugly and fast. They bury someone. They carry children. [[P1: 1:28, "be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it." Tension: dossier §4.1.]]

Dry country, and a river running north through it, green on both banks. They follow the river.

### 11. Rest (4:10–4:35)

They come up onto the edge of a cliff and stop.

For the first time since the beginning, nothing is sped up and nothing is cut. Wind. Their breathing. Below them the river opens like a hand into many channels and spreads across a flat green plain to the horizon, with mist lying over the water in the early light. Nobody has planted anything down there. [[P1: 2:1–3, rest; 2:5–6, no one to work the ground, and the *'ed* that waters the whole face of it. P2: the Mokattam escarpment at the head of the delta.]]

The band stands and looks. Nobody speaks.

We move in, slowly, on one of them. The face is one we've seen before, at the very start, in the static.

The face draws one breath. [[P1: 2:7, "and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life."]]

Closer: the eye, with the river in it.

Black.
