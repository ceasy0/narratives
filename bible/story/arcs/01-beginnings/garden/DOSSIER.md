## 2. The look

**The rule for the whole Garden (yours):** "find the line between natural and too rare to be natural; I think that's where the magic is here." The plan is [layout-plan-ring.svg](./layout-plan-ring.svg).

- **Where:** a round island, about 450 m across, in a ring of fresh water 30–60 m wide, out in the marsh: a stranger in the plain, "unordinary but not impossible." Beyond it the marsh, the plain, the sea on the horizon. ([LAYOUT §1–2](./LAYOUT.md#2-the-island-and-the-ring))
- **The water:** an artesian spring rises on the open top between the two trees. Four headwaters run straight to the four directions, cross the ring as current seams, and leave through a low rim of spring stone as natural rivers (2:10). ([§3](./LAYOUT.md#3-the-water-the-spring-the-headwaters-and-the-rivers))
- **The centre:** five broad, low terraces of old grey spring stone, built by the spring. They're round at the foot and squared toward the top by Adam's work ("to work it and keep it," 2:15). The top is an open pavement that catches the sun. ([§4](./LAYOUT.md#4-the-stone-and-the-terraces))
- **The stone:** grey limestone, natural pavement, never cut or carved.
- **The paths:** broad limestone slabs along both banks of each headwater. Clear paths of earth, gravel, sand and stepping stones wind through the four planted quarters. ([§5](./LAYOUT.md#5-the-paths-and-the-quarters))
- **The trees:** the tree of life to the north, the tree of knowledge to the south, so the way in from the east runs straight up between them. They look different from each other, but neither looks strange (3:6). ([§6.1](./LAYOUT.md#61-the-two-trees-life-north-knowledge-south))
- **Plants and animals:** of the region or the wider region, the whole region in one place; nothing from elsewhere. ([§6.2](./LAYOUT.md#62-the-plants-and-animals))
- **Light and air:** sunny yet cool and breezy, never hot and humid. The air is dry and clear, with a breeze off the water, in the plain's spring season. The shade makes the blue; most of the frame is in shade, with warm shafts of sun. Outside after 3:24 is the plain's summer. ([§7.1](./LAYOUT.md#71-the-light))
- **Color:** stone grey and forest green, with color only in what lives, plus light, water and sky. **Blue and yellow** are drawn out wherever they come naturally. Nothing made is colored; the ancestor's off-white linen is the only near-white thing. ([§7.2](./LAYOUT.md#72-color-grey-and-green-with-blue-and-yellow))
- **Gold:** the evening's gold light (3:8), golden bdellium resin on a few *Commiphora* trees (2:12), and gold in living things. There's no gold in the water and nothing is gilded. ([§7.3](./LAYOUT.md#73-gold))
- **One man's work:** artificial enough that the audience almost thinks it isn't natural, natural enough that one man could have done it with time. It already looks kept when Adam first walks in, as God's planting made ready for him. ([§8](./LAYOUT.md#8-one-mans-work))
- **The way east:** two limestone slab bridges on stacked-stone piers, one each side of the eastern river, with the ring flowing on under them. Earth paths run along both banks outside. It's the only dry way in and out, where Adam first stands (2:8) and where the cherubim and the fire stand (3:24). ([§9](./LAYOUT.md#9-the-way-east-two-slab-bridges))
- **Eridu and Babel:** Eridu is built after the expulsion (about 5400 BC) on the dune at the Garden's gate, and Babel's tower is Eridu's. ([§1.2](./LAYOUT.md#12-the-date-about-5500-bc-before-eridu), [§1.4](./LAYOUT.md#14-babel-is-eridu))

## 3. The six movements

Where an ancestor detail is still open (item 13), the movement is written on the pick.

| # | Movement | Text | About | What fills it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The breath let out | 2:8–9, 15–17 | 8 min | Adam's first shot: the breath from the cliff let out, at the east entrance where the bridges reach the island. A man in his early twenties, a slight, pleasant wonder on his face. Taken and put: the ancestor brings him to the gate and lets him walk in alone. The walk to the centre ([LAYOUT §10](./LAYOUT.md#10-the-walk-in)): no talking, no music or very little, only birds, water, wind and the animals' small sounds. He goes up the eastern headwater and through the quarters, past all the animals, which look up and go back to grazing, drinking and resting (he passes them, not meets them: that's the naming). The second register is at work (a sign, a double meaning, nothing explained). The centre; the climb; the two trees. The world goes quiet; the held note; the command, the first words of the series, from the ancestor waiting at the trees. |
| 2 | The naming | 2:18–20 | 10 min | The animals come, one long day, along the paths: he and each animal look at each other until he knows it; we never hear a name. Wonder, comedy, and then "no helper opposite him": loneliness, earned by the looking. |
| 3 | The sleep and the woman | 2:21–25 | 7 min | The deep sleep: the Garden's first glimpse of the Opening, one face becoming two. He wakes and she's beside him, of one kind; the ancestor has brought her (2:22b). The poem (2:23), exactly. "Naked and not ashamed." |
| 4 | Their life | — | 10 min | The work and the keeping, the seasons, the river, the animals, the two trees seen and not discussed, the outside seen once from the edge as a frontier. Mostly wordless: the Opening's grammar in a garden, and the second register. His work on the centre (the squared top). Any foreshadowing you want goes here. |
| 5 | The serpent | 3:1–7 | 8 min | The climb; the question from outside, with the hiss under it; the fruit; the eyes; the leaves. |
| 6 | The sound in the wind | 3:8–24 | 12 min | The wind through the Garden and the two of them going still: the ancestor's footsteps (3:8), and they hide from his face. Then the questions and the sentences, the serpent's too; the name; the dead animal, and his hands making the clothes; "like one of us" (3:22), to no one. The way east, down, over the bridges; he stays inside the gate. The Opening's world at the gate, and the animals' faces in the fire. |
